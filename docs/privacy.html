Privacy Policy for Avy Tab Switcher

Avy Tab Switcher accesses metadata of currently open browser tabs, including tab titles, URLs, favicons, and recent access information.

This information is used only to provide the extension's tab-switching and tab-search functionality.

  • Avy Tab Switcher does not transmit tab information to external services.
  • It does not sell or share user data.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
  • It does not inspect or collect website page content.
  • It does not collect personally identifiable information.

All tab metadata is processed locally in the user's browser.
